===============================================================================
 NOTES
===============================================================================

- The --no-verify-jwt flag disables JWT verification on the functions, which
  allows the ESP32 firmware to call them with just the service role key.
- The --use-api flag bundles the functions server-side (no Docker needed).
- The supabase/ directory at the project root was created by "supabase init"
  and contains copies of the functions in the expected CLI directory structure.
- Functions are also stored at: supabase-firmware/backend/functions/
